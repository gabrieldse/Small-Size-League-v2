# Day 5: Develop and Test Your Strategy

Today is the final day of your onboarding! It is time to put everything together.

## The Final Challenge

Your goal is to develop a custom strategy and test it in the simulation.

1. **Plan your strategy**: Define how your robots should position themselves, pass the ball, or handle defense.
2. **Implement**: Code the changes in the `strategy` library.
3. **Test in Simulation**: Launch `grSim` and watch your strategy play out.
4. **Refine**: Fix bugs and improve your structural logic based on visual observation.

Once your strategy is working continuously without panicking, you can make a pull request so we can evaluate your development. After implementing the corrections, you will have successfully completed onboarding. You are ready to start collaborating and climbing the project rankings!
